About Me

I'm Dhruv. CS grad student at USC, graduating May 2026. I got into this because I played a lot of games and kept wondering how they worked. Six years later I'm writing C++ and CUDA and building things that render in real time. I TA a game engine course here at USC — which is kind of surreal because I was sitting in that class a year ago. Looking for full-time work when I graduate. If that's you, let's talk.

What I Do

Systems & Performance

C++ and making things fast. I've spent a lot of time figuring out why something is slow and fixing it — whether that's GPU kernel design, bad memory access patterns, or just too much happening at once. I like this kind of work.

Software Engineering

I've worked on teams where someone else had to read my code and build on it. That changes how you write. I care about clean structure — not over-engineered, just clear. Enough that the next person doesn't have to ask you what it does.

Graphics & Interactive Tech

Real-time 3D is the main thing. Shaders, rendering pipelines, getting something to look right and run fast at the same time. I've also built XR apps on Meta Quest 3. Honestly the best part of this job is when you write something and can immediately see it running.
